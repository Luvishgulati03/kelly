Explore Kelly

Three ways in

Talk, Counter, Chat

Pick a mode and have a real conversation with Kelly. Your conversation stays in your browser session and is not saved.

Recommended

Talk

hands-free voice

A natural back-and-forth, like talking to a person at the counter. Kelly listens, notices when you pause, and answers out loud.

When shops use it: a tablet on the counter during busy hours, so customers can ask while staff keep their hands free.

How to converse

  1. Tap Start and allow the microphone.
  2. Just speak naturally, in English, Hindi or Hinglish.
  3. Pause, and Kelly replies out loud.
  4. Interrupt her anytime by speaking again.
  5. Say show me designs to open the design slideshow.

        Speech is recognised and spoken on the owner's own Mac, with no cloud speech service. The only thing Kelly trusts for prices is the owner's reviewed catalogue.
